# Results Wizard Outreach Pack

Updated: 2026-05-03

## One-Line Offer

Results Wizard helps schools send parents official result links at the end of term, then turns the same results batch into aggregate analytics for leadership and school marketing.

## Short Positioning

Schools already prepare results every term. Results Wizard makes that work easier by turning marksheets, report cards, spreadsheets, and exam summaries into:

- private parent result pages
- review-before-publish workflows
- parent link tracking
- aggregate school analytics
- public-safe performance snapshots

PLE is one useful source format, but the product is a broader school results portal.

## Pilot Offer

**Term Results Portal Pilot**

For one school term, Results Wizard will help a school:

1. Prepare a reviewed results batch.
2. Preview private parent result pages.
3. Send or demonstrate official parent result links.
4. Produce an aggregate analytics summary for school leadership.
5. Identify the custom report-card or analytics setup the school needs next.

Suggested pilot price test:

- UGX 300,000-800,000 for one term/results batch
- UGX 450,000+ where custom analytics and setup are included

## Demo Script

1. Open the launch page: "This is Results Wizard: official parent result links plus school analytics."
2. Open the demo portal dashboard: "The school sees the term, result batches, review issues, parent link activity, and analytics."
3. Open New Batch: "The school uploads a marksheet, spreadsheet, PDF, pasted table, or PLE file."
4. Open Review: "Teachers review missing marks, comments, duplicates, and errors before anything is published."
5. Open Parent View: "This is what a parent receives on mobile: official school identity, result ID, subjects, comments, and correction path."
6. Open Links: "The school can send, track, revoke, or print parent access codes."
7. Open Analytics: "Leadership gets aggregate insights without exposing individual learner data."
8. Open Subscription: "The base portal handles result links; the analytics subscription turns results into reports for decisions and marketing."

## Lead Qualification Questions

- How do you currently prepare and share end-of-term results?
- Do parents receive printed slips, WhatsApp messages, email, SMS, or a portal?
- Which formats do you use: Excel marksheets, PDFs, report cards, PLE summaries, or school management software exports?
- Do you need positions/ranks shown to parents, or should those be hidden?
- Do you use CBC-style competency reports?
- Who approves results before parents see them?
- Would aggregate analytics be useful for leadership meetings, parent meetings, or school marketing?
- How many learners would be included in a typical term batch?
